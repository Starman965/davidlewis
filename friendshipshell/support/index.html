Before you write

Please include your device model, operating system version, and what happened. A screenshot can help, but please avoid including a child’s name, photo, or other personal information.

Common questions

What devices is the game for?

Friendship Shell is designed for Mac and iPad. Check the App Store listing for supported operating system versions when the game is available.

Are pearls purchased with real money?

No. Pearls are earned through play. The complete game is a one-time purchase, with no in-app purchases or subscription.

What is the difference between Adventure and Explore?

Start Adventure follows Naomi’s story. Explore opens the ocean for free play, discovery, and decorating without starting the story.

Where is Krabby Catapult?

Find the game while exploring under the sea. After your first catapult launch, a shortcut appears on Naomi’s tablet at home.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is stored on the device. Cross-device progress syncing is not provided. Deleting the app or its saved data may remove your progress.

I cannot hear the dialogue or music.

Check your device volume and the game’s sound setting in the pause menu. If you use headphones, check that audio is playing through the intended output.

How do refunds work?

Apple handles App Store purchases and refund requests. Visit Apple’s purchase support to review your purchase.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or information identifying a child.
