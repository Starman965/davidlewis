Privacy, plainly explained

Privacy policy

Friendship Shell is designed for play without a player account, advertising, or tracking.

Effective November 13, 2026. This policy covers the Friendship Shell game and these game information pages, operated by David Lewis.

Information inside the game

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to David Lewis. It does not include advertising, analytics SDKs, social chat, or third-party tracking. It does not ask children to provide their name, email address, location, photographs, or recordings.

Saved progress

Story progress, earned pearls, unlocked activities, and settings are stored locally on the device. These records are used to continue play and are not sent to a developer server. The game does not provide cross-device progress syncing. Your device’s backup settings may include app data.

Purchases and Apple services

The complete game is offered as a one-time App Store purchase. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple processes purchases and may process information through its own services, including diagnostics where enabled in your device settings, under Apple’s privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Contacting support

If a parent or guardian emails support, we receive the email address and information they choose to send. We use that information to answer the request and resolve the issue, not for advertising. Please do not include identifying information about a child. You may ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the contact below, subject to any applicable retention requirements.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The game pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies and do not load third-party trackers. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following links to Apple or another website is subject to that service’s privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game. Support is intended for parents and guardians. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game’s data practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions or support correspondence deletion requests, contact Demandgendave@gmail.com.
