Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 30, 2025

Thank you for using Wrist Score. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Wrist Score is designed with your privacy in mind. All data is stored locally on your devices, including:

  • Game scores, serving team, and server number
  • Simple stats such as games played and win percentage
  • Color and layout preferences you choose in Settings

How We Use Your Information

The information you enter in Wrist Score:

  • Is stored locally on your device
  • Is not transmitted to our servers
  • Is not shared with third parties
  • Is only used to provide you with the app's functionality

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your devices using Apple's system storage APIs. The data persists until you choose to delete it via in‑app reset options or uninstall the app.

iCloud Integration

Currently, Wrist Score does not use iCloud or any cloud storage services. All data remains on your local devices.

Analytics

We do not collect any analytics or tracking information about your use of the app.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data. You can:

  • View all data stored in the app
  • Delete individual games or all data at any time
  • Remove the app and all associated data from your device

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at demandgendave@gmail.com

California Privacy Rights

The app does not collect or share any personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

International Users

The app complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) by design, as it does not collect, process, or transfer any personal data outside of your device.
