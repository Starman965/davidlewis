02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Hi, I’m David.
A builder at heart.

Technology has always made me ask,
“What could we do with this?”

After more than 35 years in B2B marketing and technology, including founding DemandGen International and selling it to BDO USA in 2021, I’m exploring that question in a whole new way.

Now I use AI to build software, dream up games, create stories, and make everyday life more productive and enjoyable. Sometimes the inspiration is a pickleball match. Sometimes it’s a practical problem. And sometimes it’s a granddaughter who loves mermaids.

I’m also the author of Manufacturing Demand, a podcaster, YouTuber, and speaker. I enjoy helping people discover what they can make, too.
