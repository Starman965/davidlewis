01 / Start here

AI for Newbies

45 minutes + 15 minutes of Q&A

Your phone just became a lot more useful.

Turn “Where do I start?” into “What else can I do?” Discover five practical ways to use ChatGPT, with simple demonstrations you can try before dinner.

  • Get advice that fits your situation.
  • Create photos and find the right words.
  • Type, talk, or show AI what you mean.
Five things you can do today
  1. Get a thinking partner. Explore travel choices, work through a tricky conversation, or prepare questions for your doctor—with context, follow-up questions, and a second perspective.
  2. Create and fix photos. Turn an idea into an image, remove a distraction, or give a favorite picture a fresh look.
  3. Find the right words. Draft an email, shape a proposal, or write a speech that sounds like you.
  4. Make a plan you’ll actually use. Build a day in port, a dinner menu, or a packing list around your preferences.
  5. Learn by showing and asking. Explore a new topic, upload a photo, or use voice and supported live-video features to talk through what you’re looking at.

Along the way: what to share, what to double-check, and which features depend on your plan or device.

Leave with five reasons to open ChatGPT tomorrow.

AI in Daily Life

45 minutes + 15 minutes of Q&A

That “someday” idea? Give it a first version.

Turn a memory into a song, a trip into a short film, or a small annoyance into a tool of your own. Discover how to get from an idea to a first version you can actually use.

  • Build a personal planner, tracker, or game.
  • Create music, videos, and narration.
  • Choose the right tools for your first project.
See what you could make
  • A tool made for your life. Explore a hobby tracker, family trip planner, or simple game, starting with a plain-English description.
  • A song with a personal story. Turn a memory or celebration into lyrics and music with Suno.
  • A video worth sharing. Explore bringing scenes and ideas to life with Higgsfield, then adding narration with ElevenLabs.
  • A creative process you can repeat. Use ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok to develop ideas, refine drafts, and work through the next step.

Learn where each tool fits, what takes practice, and where paid features may come into play.

Leave with a project you’re excited to start.

03 / Put AI to work

AI Agents

45 minutes + 15 minutes of Q&A

What would you hand off if you had an assistant?

Imagine help that already knows your preferences. Learn how to set up an AI helper with a clear job, useful context, and boundaries that keep you in charge.

  • Plan trips around your interests and pace.
  • Organize your day and prepare replies.
  • Support your wellness goals and routines.
Meet your possible support team
  • Your travel planner. Research destinations, compare options, and prepare itineraries around your pace, interests, and budget.
  • Your executive assistant. Turn scattered information into a daily briefing, draft replies, and organize next steps—with supported connections and your approval.
  • Your health and habit coach. Organize wellness goals, prepare check-ins, and support everyday routines alongside professional care.
  • Your first custom agent. Define its role, give it useful context, test it on a small task, and decide what requires your review.

Understand the difference between a reusable assistant and an agent that takes actions. You stay in charge of access, decisions, and spending.

Leave knowing what to delegate—and how to begin.

04 / Get better results

Prompt Like a Pro

45 minutes + 15 minutes of Q&A

Less “That’s not it.” More “That’s exactly what I wanted.”

The difference between a generic answer and something you love often starts with the brief. Learn how to tell AI what matters, give it the right context, and guide it toward a result worth using—from a beautiful image to a trip that feels made for you.

  • Turn a vague request into a clear creative brief.
  • Build a trip around your hotels, activities, and dining preferences.
  • Use Projects, Skills, and clear goals to guide repeatable work.
From “almost” to “exactly”
  • Start with the result you want. Give AI a goal, relevant context, constraints, and an example of a good result. Ask it to clarify what’s missing before it starts.
  • Get the image you have in mind. Describe the subject, setting, composition, mood, and intended use. Then refine specific details instead of starting over with another vague request.
  • Plan a trip that feels like you. Build a sequence of prompts: share your dates, budget, interests, and pace; compare hotels; shape each day around activities and dining; then check travel times, current availability, and the details that matter.
  • Give your AI the background. Use Projects to bring together documents, preferences, and instructions. Your booking confirmations and travel notes become useful context for planning.
  • Teach it a process with Skills. Explore reusable instructions in tools that support Skills—so a favorite itinerary format or review checklist can guide future tasks.
  • Brief an agent, then review its work. Define the goal, deliverable, boundaries, and approval points. Learn how to steer an assistant through a task and check whether the result meets your brief.

See how Projects, Skills, and goal-setting work together. Features vary by tool; the habit of giving clear direction travels with you.

Leave with a repeatable way to ask—and get more useful results.
