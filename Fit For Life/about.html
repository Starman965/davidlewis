Fit For Life

Comprehensive Fitness & Nutrition Tracker

About Fit For Life

Fit For Life is a comprehensive fitness and nutrition tracking app with AI-powered food analysis. Track your workouts, monitor your macronutrients, sync with Apple HealthKit, and achieve your fitness goals with personalized insights and progress tracking.

🤖

AI Food Analysis

Take a photo of your food and let AI automatically identify items and calculate nutritional information.

❤️

HealthKit Integration

Seamlessly sync with Apple Health for comprehensive fitness data including steps, workouts, and weight.

🏋️

Exercise Tracking

Log strength training exercises with sets, reps, and weight. Upload photos to track your form and progress.

📊

Macro Tracking

Monitor protein, carbohydrates, and fats with visual progress bars and daily goal tracking.

⚖️

Weight Management

Track your weight over time with beautiful charts showing progress toward your goals.

🎯

Personalized Goals

Set custom calorie and macro goals based on your activity level and fitness objectives.

🔐

Firebase Sync

Cloud synchronization with Firebase ensures your data is safe and accessible across devices.

🌙

Dark Mode

Beautiful dark theme that's easy on the eyes with customizable appearance settings.

How It Works

  1. Create Your Profile

    Set up your account with Firebase Authentication and configure your fitness goals and macro targets.

  2. Log Your Nutrition

    Snap a photo of your meal for AI analysis or manually enter food items with nutritional info.

  3. Track Your Workouts

    Record strength training sessions with exercise details, sets, reps, and photos of your form.

  4. Monitor Progress

    View your dashboard to see macro progress, weight trends, and workout history at a glance.

  5. Sync with Health

    Connect with Apple HealthKit to import weight data and sync your fitness activities.

Technical Implementation

Built with SwiftUI and modern iOS frameworks, Fit For Life leverages cutting-edge technology to provide a seamless fitness tracking experience.

Framework

SwiftUI with iOS 16+ features

AI Integration

Health Sync

Apple HealthKit integration

Backend

Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Auth)

Smart Sync System

Fit For Life features an intelligent synchronization system that seamlessly merges data from Apple HealthKit with manual entries. The app prioritizes HealthKit data when available while preserving your manual logs, ensuring accurate and comprehensive fitness tracking.
