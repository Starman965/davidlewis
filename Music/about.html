Featured Release

The Start of My Song is my first released track — a love song written for my wife, Tiffanie, in the spirit of our wedding song by Luther Vandross, with vocal moments that nod to Michael Bolton. I wrote the lyrics and used AI and extensive prompting to craft the instruments, vocals, and melody, as I do with all my songs. It’s available now on Spotify and streaming platforms.
